About

Why GLAMSHUB exists

Libraries, archives, museums and galleries hold the memory of communities. When they are shelled, burned, looted or left to decay, the loss is often reported once, in passing, and then scattered across thousands of news pages.

What we do

GLAMSHUB gathers public news reports about damage to, destruction of and looting from GLAM institutions (galleries, libraries, archives and museums) anywhere in the world. We keep one structured record per incident, with a date, a place, the type of institution, the cause, and a link to the original report, so that losses can be searched, mapped and counted.

What counts

  • Institutions: public, private, university, school, national, community and religious libraries, archives, museums and galleries, including their collections.
  • Causes: war and armed conflict, looting, deliberate destruction, fire, flood, earthquake, structural collapse and neglect.
  • Outcomes: destroyed, damaged, looted, or at risk.

Automatic, then reviewed

A scheduled job searches news sources several times a day, filters for relevant reports, removes duplicates and publishes new records labelled Automated, unverified. People then review records and mark them Reported or Verified. Read the methodology for the details and limits.

Take part

If you work in a library, archive, museum or gallery, or study heritage in conflict, you can report an incident or suggest a correction on any record. Everything, including the code and the data, lives in the GitHub repository.

Related organisations

GLAMSHUB is an independent project and is not affiliated with the organisations below. They do authoritative work on cultural heritage protection, and their own reports should be preferred where available: UNESCO, ICOM, IFLA, the International Council on Archives (ICA), the Blue Shield, and ALIPH.
